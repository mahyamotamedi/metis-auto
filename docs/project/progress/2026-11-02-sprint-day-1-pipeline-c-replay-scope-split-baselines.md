- **Rules:** `period` comes from `a_timestamp` with inclusive naive bounds; a missing time or one outside both periods is `unknown`. `llm_request` is False only for `bot_provider_name == "workflow"`; a missing provider is refused (unknown is not False). The split is for fitting data. A whole session takes the period of its first timed request. A duplicate family (`bot_id`, `u_h_norm`, rows with user text) on both sides goes to the side with more of its rows, and its other rows become `excluded`. A bot with one request is always insufficient. The tenant cap keeps whole sessions of the capped tenant in a seeded order. As-logged is in recorded units (blocks `fees` and `token_cost`). The three repriced baselines are in price-file units and carry `token_cost` only. Unpriced models count as unknown, not zero. Cached tokens are added back for the models that report them separately.
