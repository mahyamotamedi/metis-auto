## Reranker cut (R-A2) and overhead per version (L-1) (2026-11-02)

Final-plan tasks L-1 and R-A2 (Pipeline A), branch `reranker-overhead` (from `sprint-saba`). Status: **awaiting review**; nothing committed. No model calls, no GPU, no live calls.

- **L-1 (`scripts/measure_lever_overhead.py`):** loads a version through `wiring.experiments.load_candidate`, times `apply` with `perf_counter_ns` on 1,000 seeded report-period LLM rows, one row per call (`apply` takes frames), after one warm-up call. It prints p50, p95 and max, the hardware and the 100 ms p95 verdict (Q-14), and writes `overhead_ms_p50` / `overhead_ms_p95` into every `reports/levers/` result of that `lever_id`. It adds the assumption "offline per-request estimate on a local machine; not a load test (Q-14)" and a caveat line. The manifest and every other field stay unchanged (tested). A version that raises writes nothing.
- **L-1 results** (AMD Ryzen 7 5800H, CPython 3.13.9), p95 in ms, all **pass**: popular-question `first_turn` 51.8, `all_turns` 63.1, `first_turn.ttl24h` 52.1, `all_turns.ttl24h` 52.8 (16 R-A1 files). Reranker cut: `observed_relevancy` 86.4, `lexical_overlap` 96.5. For the cache, a one-row frame has an empty nightly window, so this times eligibility, keying and the guard, not the nightly build or a lookup in a built set.
- **R-A2 (`experiments/context/exp-ctx-003-reranker-cut/`, `scripts/run_reranker_cut.py`):** `RerankerCut(scorer, floor, threshold, top_k)`, a context `StepVersion`. **S-6 runs first:** exact copies (same `h_exact`; `chunk_id` ignored) are collapsed with S-6's rule, read from `origin/context-within-message-dedup` without merging (it matches the owner's rule). Unique chunks take the maximum of their copies' scores; a missing score is kept and counted. Tokens = characters × 0.3384 (EDA `recon.t0rag.coef_rag`), from uncached input. Cost is repriced at `todays_models`; recorded cost is unchanged.
- **Step 0 findings:** `chunks.message_id` is the AI answer and joins `requests.ai_message_id` exactly (63,415 requests; row and character counts match). There is no missing relevancy, and scores run 0 to 1, best first per RAG query. 36.2% of eligible chunk rows (38.8% of characters) repeat inside a request. Eligible report-period requests: 16,099 (65.4% of LLM requests, 92.4% of recorded cost).
- **R-A2 results (report period, tokens only, quality not measured):**
  - `observed_relevancy` (top_k 12, threshold q25, floor 3, chosen on tune): of 140.3 M raw chunk tokens, dedup (S-6, not counted) removes 59.4 M and R-A2 on top removes **18.6 M**. That is 23.1% of unique chunk characters, with 91.3% of the score mass kept. Repriced cost share saved 4.83% (95% CI 3.11% to 6.69%).
  - `lexical_overlap` (2,000-request sample; no top_k, threshold q50): R-A2 removes 2.3 M of 17.6 M raw chunk tokens; cost share 8.15% (4.84% to 13.08%).
  - Curve: [exp-ctx-003 README](../../../experiments/context/exp-ctx-003-reranker-cut/README.md#results-on-the-full-data-2026-11-02) and `reports/levers/context.reranker_cut.curve.csv`.
- **Main caveat:** `gemini-2.5-flash` (90% of eligible requests, about 95% of their recorded cost) is not in `catalogue-metis-test.toml`. The repriced cost shares (here and in R-A1's `todays_models` files) therefore cover a small, unrepresentative subset: 1,495 of 16,099 eligible requests for the observed scorer. Token numbers cover every eligible request.
