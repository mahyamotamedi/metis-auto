# Experiment plan from the full-dataset EDA

Minimal, budget-aware experiments that turn the EDA's candidate numbers into evidence. **All are pending**; none has been run. Thresholds are the provisional ADR-0006 ones (judge score drop at most 3% overall and at most 5% per stratum or language, relative to the baseline mean; pipeline overhead at most 100 ms p95), to be revisited with this data. Any live call follows ADR-0018 (10 USD lifetime cap, owner approval per run, sample conversations need per-run approval) and ADR-0003 (offline by default). Candidate code lives in `experiments/` until promoted (ADR-0009, ADR-0022).

Common rules: split by whole session; temporal holdout (tune on the earliest part, test on the latest, using naive timestamps with the caveat in Q-06); control exact and near-duplicate families so they do not straddle splits; freeze thresholds before the final test; report every rate with n and a session-clustered interval; a representative set (for workload estimates) is kept apart from a stress set (long contexts, JSON answers, attachments, high-cost bots).

| ID | Question | Inputs | Method | Metrics | Acceptance (provisional) | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| X1 | Are exact-cache candidate hits valid? | a sample of full-key and bot-plus-text candidate hits from the replay; answers from the log | human review of "does the cached answer satisfy the new request", stratified by key variant, scope, turn, memory, RAG | hit correctness with interval; stale or personalised share | agreed correctness floor on the full-key variant before any serving; bot-plus-text stays measurement-only | pending (review set to be built from candidate hits) |
| X2 | Do per-call fees fall if retrieval queries are cached or deduplicated? | RAG queries per message; fee items | replay: cache retrieval by normalised query per bot with TTL; count fee items avoided; review retrieval equivalence | fee items avoided, share of recorded cost, retrieval correctness | positive net saving after lookup cost, no drop in groundedness on a reviewed subset | pending |
| X3 | Semantic cache: precision and incremental hits beyond exact | `semantic_pair_validity.csv` (lexical candidate pairs), plus embedding candidates from a Persian-capable model chosen after a licence and CPU-latency check | label safe, unsafe or uncertain with hard negatives (changed numbers, negation, state, schema); tune the threshold on a development slice only | precision, labelled-universe recall, incorrect-hit rate, lookup latency p95 | precision target set before the test; no global-recall claim from candidate-only labels | pending (needs reviewers) |
| X4 | History window or summary: does a shorter history keep quality? | multi-turn requests on non-memory bots; model held fixed | replay original vs windowed history (recent K turns, token budget) through the configured model; judge calibrated on a small human-rated set | judge score drop, lost entities and numbers, tokens saved | within the margins; fallback to full history on dependency flags | pending (live calls or recorded replay) |
| X5 | RAG chunk deduplication and top-k reduction | requests with repeated chunks (same message or earlier in session) | full RAG vs deduplicated vs reduced k vs no RAG on the same requests | groundedness and answer quality, tokens saved | no loss against full RAG within the margins; no-RAG only as a diagnostic | pending |
| X6 | Instruction compaction for the largest groups | the largest instruction groups by request share | shorter equivalent templates checked against a rule checklist; fixed-model quality comparison; effect on provider prefix caching | tokens saved, rule violations, cached share before and after | no rule loss; net of lost prefix caching | pending |
| X7 | Routing benchmark | a stratified evaluation set (language, length, RAG, history depth, JSON, cost-heavy bots) answered by the candidate models on the same effective requests | rule-based router (length, language, RAG, attachments, depth) first; judge plus objective checks (schema, exact values) | acceptable-answer rate per stratum, cost per acceptable answer, escalation rate | non-inferior to the baseline within the margins; per-language check | pending (live budget decision) |
| X8 | Cascade and failover | X7 results plus new logging of attempts | cheap-first with a defined weak-answer signal (`LENGTH`, schema failure, checker score); fault injection for failover | total cost including checker, latency, failover time | cost of all attempts below the single-model baseline at equal quality | pending (needs signal and logging) |
| X9 | Combined pipeline | outputs of X1 to X8 | sequential decomposition with `AccountingService.decompose` on one workload | net savings, quality, overhead p95 | within the margins and the overhead budget | pending |

**Suggested order:** X1 and X2 first (offline, cheap, and the fee block is the largest single cost item), then X3 reviews, then X4 and X5 on recorded replays, then X7 once the live budget is decided.

**Not claimed anywhere yet:** quality preservation, production safety, exact billing, net savings.
