# reports/

Committed experiment outputs: figures and **feature-only** tables with hashed or opaque IDs. No raw content.

- Layout: `reports/<area>/<EXP-ID>/`. The full-dataset EDA goes in `reports/full_eda/`.
- `sample_eda/` is the preliminary sample EDA, kept unchanged.
- Per-run records stay in `runs/` (gitignored). Only curated summaries come here.

Rules: [repository_structure.md §7](../docs/architecture/repository_structure.md#7-reports-reports).
